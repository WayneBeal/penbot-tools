Welcome to PenBot

We'll turn your own handwriting into a font our robot can write with real ink. It takes three short steps, about 20 to 30 minutes in total, and you can stop and come back at any time. Your progress is saved on this device.

1 Letters
Write each letter, number and symbol. Write each one 3 times in the box, one under the other. A warm-up word helps you write naturally. About 15 min.
2 Sentences
Copy 13 short sentences in your normal joined-up writing so we learn how you link your letters. About 10 min.
3 Signature
Optional: sign up to 3 times, with your consent, for your own letters and cards. About 2 min.

Tip: use a stylus if you have one. With Pen only on, you can rest your hand on the screen while you write.
